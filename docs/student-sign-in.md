# Student sign-in (off by default)

Student sign-in is a separate, English-only student portal at `/student/{school}`. The school must be a school organisation with the hidden `student_sign_in` grant enabled. Existing parents, app members and parent-minted avatar child mode keep their existing authentication. This build does not enable any school. The page first checks the public, no-store `GET /api/student/masjids/{school}/availability` endpoint. Off, never-enabled and missing schools return 404 and show “Student sign-in is not available at this school.” without a sign-in form. A failed network check offers a retry.

The office opens a class and expands **Student accounts**. It chooses **Students sign in from grade**, initially Grade 6. A child must have current active class memberships with recognized `GradeLevel` grades at or above that threshold. Unknown grades and any younger current class grade refuse access. The same check runs at account issuance, sign-in and every student request. The office cannot issue credentials while the switch is off.

Enter an ASCII username, then **Create account** or **Reset password**. Usernames are case-insensitively unique within a school. A reset invalidates the previous password and every student token, then returns a new random one-time password once. Copy or print it immediately. **Done with passwords** clears the preview. No password is emailed or stored as plaintext. For a class, explicitly tick the counted reset confirmation and choose **Make class slips**; this resets all eligible pupils in that class and prepares individually printable slips. New accounts made through class slips use lowercase ASCII `first.last` usernames: accents are transliterated and other characters dropped. Each name part is capped at 19 characters to keep both names within the 40-character rule; school-local collisions append 2, 3 and so on, shortening the base as needed. Names that cannot make a three-character username fall back to `student` or `student.<remaining name>`. Existing usernames, including office edits, are kept on later slips. The batch is atomic and capped at 100. Keep slips private. **Turn off** disables an account and immediately revokes its tokens. The office sees last sign-in and can read recent sign-in outcomes at its authenticated API.

A student signs in with that username and password, then must choose a different password before opening any schoolwork. Passwords require at least 10 characters, a letter and a digit, and at most 72 bytes, matching the app's bcrypt hasher. They can later choose **Change password** or ask the office for a reset. Password change also invalidates all older tokens. Sign out revokes the active token.

Student sessions expire after 15 minutes without an authenticated request and after 120 minutes regardless of activity. The parent session's absolute window is 30 days. Logout/expiry clears cached records and password/file inputs. Delayed responses, prepared photos and downloads from a previous account or selection are discarded. Its HTTP client uses a distinct, school-specific sessionStorage key and explicitly replaces inherited Authorization headers. It never reads family, child-mode or staff storage. Opening a student page directly skips staff bootstrap.

The student sees only their own current classes and teacher names, their dated timetable from the existing timetable reader, work and due dates, the family mark reader's arithmetic and assignment projection, published family letter grades, and the school calendar. No classmate list, staff note, report comment, override provenance or reason is included. A different child's membership or hand-in ID returns 404. There are no messages or posting controls. Date and time display labels are formatted on the server using `SchoolCalendarReaders::for($id)->timezone()`: due dates and timetable/closure days use `Tue, Oct 20`, year/term ranges include the year, lesson times use `9:00 AM`, and hand-in/last-sign-in times use `Oct 14, 2026, 5:17 PM`. Calendar dates are interpreted in that timezone without converting a UTC midnight; stored instants are converted to it. The student projection adds `*_label` fields alongside raw dates/times; shared calendar serializers remain unchanged. Family and office account status add `last_sign_in_label`, and shared hand-in payloads add `submitted_at_label`.

Teachers open an assignment in the gradebook and enable **Students hand in this work**, optionally with a due date. The student hands in a file, photo or http/https link. The due date is the end of that school-local date; later submissions say **Late**. Missing due date means no late flag. JPEG/PNG photos pass through the existing browser metadata-stripping helper; the server still validates sniffed MIME and size. File rules reuse `groups.resources`: PDF, Word, JPEG and PNG by default, 8 MiB maximum, private school storage. Public disks and disks with public URLs are refused. Stored filenames are random; original names use the existing attachment sanitizer. Downloads require the entire authenticated ownership chain and are attachments with private/no-store headers. No public or signed storage URL is returned. Links are escaped text rendered as external links, never fetched by the server.

Each hand-in is an immutable version containing its child contact, membership, class, work, time, kind, lateness and private attachment/link metadata. A replacement adds a version; it never overwrites or deletes the prior one. Any existing mark, including missing/excused, locks replacement. A school/assignment transaction mutex is shared with the existing teacher score-save route to serialize marking and submission. Teachers read hand-ins beside the gradebook and use its existing marking controls; their class and subject fences apply. The office can read hand-ins and their history.

Families see whether their child has an account, its username, whether it is enabled, and its last sign-in. They never receive a password or password hash. Labels use the family's existing seven languages; new non-English phrases are machine drafts awaiting fluent review.

Withdrawn children with existing accounts remain listed for office disabling and for a family that retains entitlement. Class slips exclude ineligible children. Closing an assignment's hand-in flag stops new submissions while keeping its existing history readable.

# Children's data and retention

The account stores the child/contact link, normalized username, password hash, enabled/first-change state and last successful sign-in. A sign-in log records submitted username, known child link, outcome, IP, a user agent bounded to 2,000 characters and server time; malformed/locked attempts are logged too. Passwords and request bodies are never part of that log. Failed sign-ins are limited to 5 per school/username and 50 per IP in a 15-minute cache window, including unknown usernames; successful sign-ins do not consume these limits and clear that username’s failure count. The next attempt after a limit is reached waits for that window to expire. These are initial conservative limits for a shared school network, not measured traffic. A daily `students:prune-sign-ins` command deletes logs older than 90 days. Queue work and external sends are unnecessary.

Deleting a child's login removes these separate credentials, tokens and identified sign-in logs even if the office retains the contact. Hand-ins are academic records and retain their immutable attribution/history. Restrictive foreign keys and `AcademicRecordsHeld` stop roster removal from destroying them. A contact with hand-ins cannot be merged away; a merge without hand-ins erases the source's student credentials. Staging nulls account hashes and last sign-in, anonymizes usernames and disables accounts, drops sign-in logs, and nulls hand-in links, original filenames and private paths. No new masjids columns are used.

The separate StudentAccount provider and bearer-only guard admit no web-session fallback. Only the exact student ability is accepted. Every issued secret has a student prefix inside its hashed value; removing it invalidates the token. Global middleware refuses that bearer outside `/api/student/` without adding SQL to existing OFF readers. Staff and family provider pins remain intact. A route-walk test submits a real student token to every registered verb outside that prefix, including public/mobile/web routes, and asserts refusal.

# Practice walkthrough for the later guides session

Use invented practice pupils and the school's authorized demo environment: office issuance and class slips; phone sign-in and required password change; class/timetable/marks; a file, photo and link; a late submission and replacement history; teacher marking and subsequent replacement refusal; Grade 5 refusal under the default threshold; reset and disabled account; another child's URL refused; family status without credentials; all surfaces hidden again after switching off. The guides/demo walk, actual printed output, browser geometry and deployed behavior are separate verification, not claims made by this local build.


## Switching the school off is a pause

With the switch off, every student, office and family student address answers 404 and open student sessions stop working at once. Their tokens are not deleted: if the school switches back on within a session's idle window, that session carries on. To end sessions, turn off or reset the student's account.
